# Family Recipes — Patio de bugambilia

## Mode

Operate; recipe reading is Read.

## Direction contract

The approved identity is a Mexican family patio suggested by bugambilia, warm white and foliage. Serve primarily older women and adult relatives. Use the exact approved colors and locally bundled Bitter headings / Atkinson Hyperlegible reading text. Character comes from typography and restrained color, without decorative costume, gradients or ornamental cards. Preserve existing Spanish-default / English functionality, recipe and administration behavior, server authorization and native navigation.

## Visual anchors

Text #352331; background #FCF8F6; primary #922C55; secondary #ECDDE3; accent #3E6650. White primary-button text. Meaningful headings in Bitter; body 18–19 dp, labels 17 dp, controls at least 48 dp, text scaling enabled. Sparse borders and 8–12 dp corner radii. Existing icons/logo and factual copy remain.

## Verification boundary

Native Android phone at normal and increased system font size. Inspect sign-in, drawer, family workspace, recipe list/detail/editor, ES and EN. This is a code-led design with the approved palette specimen as direction reference, not a pixel comp. No HTML/CSS detector applies. iOS is bundle checked, not native-device visually verified. Existing fixed-light palette remains; a dark identity is outside this change.
